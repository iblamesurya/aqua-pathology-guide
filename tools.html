How do you calculate the Claude Boyd ionic mineral ratio for shrimp?

The Claude Boyd ionic mineral ratio is calculated by multiplying pond salinity (ppt) by oceanic baseline multipliers: Calcium (ppm) = Salinity × 13.5, Magnesium (ppm) = Salinity × 40.0, and Potassium (ppm) = Salinity × 11.0, maintaining a critical Magnesium to Calcium ratio of at least 3.1 to 1 for osmoregulation.

What are safe TCBS agar Vibrio colony thresholds for vannamei?

Safe TCBS agar thresholds for Litopenaeus vannamei require green sucrose-negative Vibrio colonies to stay below 100 CFU/ml and total yellow colonies below 1,000 CFU/ml, with any green colony count exceeding 1,000 CFU/ml or luminescent colonies signaling critical risk of Acute Hepatopancreatic Necrosis Disease (AHPND/EMS).

Why must ionic mineral deficits be corrected before molting?

Ionic mineral deficits must be corrected before molting because penaeid shrimp actively absorb calcium, magnesium, and potassium directly from brackish water to mineralize post-ecdysis cuticles, and severe deficiencies cause fatal molting cramps, chronic soft-shell syndrome, and cannibalism during lunar molting peaks.
