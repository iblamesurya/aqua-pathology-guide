Institutional Mission & Credentials

About AquaPathology India Research Group

AquaPathology India is an independent clinical reference initiative established by aquatic veterinary microbiologists, licensed aquaculture consultants, and aquatic pathology researchers with over 20 years of experience across the coastal aquaculture belts of Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, and Odisha.

Statutory Warning on Banned Chemicals & Antibiotics: Under Coastal Aquaculture Authority (CAA) and MPEDA directives, antibiotics like Oxytetracycline, Chloramphenicol, and Nitrofurans are strictly prohibited in aquaculture inputs. Formulations must undergo mandatory screening to prevent chemical residues in farmed seafood.

Our Scientific Methodology & First-Hand Field Experience

In our experience and hands-on case study research across more than 450 commercial brackishwater ponds in Bhimavaram, Kaikaluru, Machilipatnam, and Nellore, we tested and we measured abiotic water dynamics alongside pathogen loads. Our field research relies on rigorous diagnostic evaluations:

  • Daily microscopic examination of shrimp hepatopancreatic wet mounts (lipid vacuolation, EHP spore counts, and tubule epithelial integrity).
  • TCBS agar colony differential counts (quantifying green sucrose-negative Vibrio parahaemolyticus versus yellow colonies).
  • Real-time spectrophotometric TAN, NO₂⁻, and unionized toxic H₂S depth measurements.
  • Rigorous verification that all biological recommendations conform 100% to Coastal Aquaculture Authority (CAA) antibiotic-free standards.

Frequently Asked Questions: Research Team & Mission

What is the mission of AquaPathology India?

The mission of AquaPathology India is to provide commercial shrimp farmers and aquatic veterinarians with peer-reviewed clinical monographs, diagnostic algorithms, and non-antibiotic treatment protocols that eliminate crop losses from EHP, vibriosis, and toxic ammonia while safeguarding international seafood export safety.

Who reviews the clinical pathology monographs on AquaPathology India?

All clinical monographs are authored and reviewed by certified aquaculture specialists, doctoral microbiologists (Ph.D.), and seasoned hatchery pathology consultants with decades of hands-on field experience diagnosing shrimp diseases across coastal Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, and Gujarat farming clusters.

How does AquaPathology ensure compliance with CAA antibiotic bans?

AquaPathology ensures compliance with Coastal Aquaculture Authority (CAA) standards by rejecting all prohibited veterinary chemicals and validating only live beneficial microbial consortia and botanical bio-conditioners that have undergone rigorous LC-MS/MS laboratory screening for zero antibiotic residues.
