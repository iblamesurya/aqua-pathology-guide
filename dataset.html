Frequently Asked Questions: Clinical Dataset & Methodology

What is the Indian Shrimp Aquaculture Pathology Benchmark Dataset?

The Indian Shrimp Aquaculture Pathology Benchmark Dataset is an open-access clinical reference documenting 12 major penaeid shrimp pathologies, abiotic water quality triggers, gazetted prohibited antibiotics, and field-validated Coastal Aquaculture Authority (CAA) approved biological probiotic regimens across Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu.

How are clinical treatment protocols validated in this dataset?

Treatment protocols are validated through paired split-pond commercial field trials across 12 intensive farming clusters in Andhra Pradesh, monitoring daily water parameters, hepatopancreatic histology, microbial CFU plate counts, and final harvest survival without administering banned veterinary antibiotics.

Which banned antibiotics are cataloged in the dataset?

The dataset catalogs 20 prohibited veterinary compounds gazetted by the Coastal Aquaculture Authority and MPEDA, including Chloramphenicol, Nitrofurans, Oxytetracycline, and Fluoroquinolones, documenting their biological inefficacy against microsporidia and severe consignment rejection risks under international export standards.

Statutory Warning on Banned Chemicals & Antibiotics: Under Coastal Aquaculture Authority (CAA) and MPEDA directives, antibiotics like Oxytetracycline, Chloramphenicol, and Nitrofurans are strictly prohibited in aquaculture inputs. Formulations must undergo mandatory screening to prevent chemical residues in farmed seafood.
